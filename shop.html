How ordering works

  1. Pick product type — print or specialty mug.
  2. Choose a scene from the gallery photos, then size / mug style.
  3. Enter shipping details and submit the form so I get your order by email .
  4. Pay securely with Stripe (and optional PayPal / Venmo if listed).

How you’ll hear from me — and how I get notified

  • Your email: orders go to hello@floridanatureprints.com (FormSubmit). Confirm once in that inbox the first time.
  • Text / phone alert: optional SMS via your carrier’s free email-to-text address in js/config.js → smsAlertEmail (e.g. 5551234567@vtext.com for Verizon).
  • Stripe: turn on payment emails/push in your Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Notifications (and the Stripe app on your phone).
