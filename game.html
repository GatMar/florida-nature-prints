Wildlife Match

Flip two cards at a time. Match the same Florida bird, alligator, or wetland neighbor. Fifteen levels, each a little harder.

  • Level 1 starts with 7 matches (14 cards)
  • Each level adds one more pair, up to 20 pairs
  • Each pair is two of the same picture
  • Every match shows points right on the board
  • Clear all 15 levels for a gift certificate: a jar of 15 shark teeth, or credit toward your next order

How to play

Tap a card, then tap another. Same picture? That’s a match — you keep the pair and earn points. Different? They flip back. Early levels give you a short peek. Later levels add a timer and look-alike birds and gators. Your progress stays on this device.
