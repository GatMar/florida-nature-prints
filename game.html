Gator Life

50 short Florida wetland levels · one gator hero · comic quiz pop-ups

  • Pixel gator on real Florida photo backgrounds from this site
  • Jump gaps, dodge wildlife hazards, grab snacks
  • Meme quiz pop-ups from NPCs (rattlers, hawks, and more)
  • Your custom baby gator art that grows bigger with age
  • Labyrinth floors, ladders, snake-ropes, asteroids
  • Cartoon speech-bubble quizzes like your reference image
  • Huge question pool (2500+) so replays stay fresh
  • Kid gator voice + Florida owl buddy cheering each step
  • Progress saves on this device

Move with arrows / WASD or the on-screen pads. Jump with Space or Z. On ladders and snake-ropes, use UP/DOWN (or W/S) to climb between floors.
Reach the green flag on an upper story. Yellow ? blocks open meme quizzes. Voice prefers American English when available.
