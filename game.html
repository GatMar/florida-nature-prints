Gator Life

Swim Florida’s swirly river labyrinth — eat fish, climb hills, dodge big gators.

  • Match & eat fish pairs in order (level 1 = 10 fish / 5 pairs)
  • Tap LOG or ROCK tiles, then an adjacent tile to reshape the path
  • Swim rivers, climb hills & roads · grow as you eat
  • Dodge BIG gators — splat OUCH if they catch you

Move: swipe or D-pad (arrows / WASD on desktop).
Match fish: each fish has a colored number. Eat one, then its match next (level 1 has 5 pairs / 10 fish).
Move tiles: tap a LOG or ROCK, then tap an adjacent square to place it to your advantage.
Avoid BIG gators — splat + OUCH! if caught.
