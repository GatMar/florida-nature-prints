Privacy

A short, plain note about what this site does when you visit.

Visit counts

Once it is switched on, this site uses GoatCounter to keep simple visit counts, such as how many times a page or a photo was viewed. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not collect personal data.

No ad tracking, no selling of data

There is no tracking for ads on this site, and no data about you is sold.
